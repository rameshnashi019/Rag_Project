PRODUCT: CP-420 Electric Coolant Pump
COMPONENT: Pump Housing
MATERIAL: Polymer Grade B
SUPPLIER: Supplier-Z
RELATED TEST: TR-1845

1. PROBLEM
Two validation samples developed housing cracks during extended thermal-cycle testing. Leakage was observed during subsequent pressure testing.

2. INVESTIGATION
Visual inspection located cracks near a molded transition adjacent to the sealing region. Sectioning and microscopy showed crack initiation from the inner surface. Dimensional measurements before testing were within drawing requirements.

3. CONTRIBUTING FACTORS
The investigation identified a combination of:
- repeated thermal expansion/contraction,
- local geometric stress concentration,
- reduced material margin after prolonged coolant/temperature exposure,
- lot-to-lot material-property variation requiring supplier investigation.

4. ROOT-CAUSE ASSESSMENT
The failure was assessed as a thermal-aging-related durability weakness in the tested material/design/process combination. The analysis did not conclude that Polymer Grade B would fail in every housing design.

5. ACTIONS
Supplier-Z material consistency investigation initiated.
Additional incoming material controls requested.
Housing geometry reviewed for local stress reduction.
Extended thermal-cycle testing added to future material-change reviews.
