DOCUMENT ID: FA-2517
DATE: 2025-08-08
PRODUCT: CP-600 Electric Coolant Pump
COMPONENT: Pump Housing
RELATED WARRANTY CASE: WR-2025-8821

1. OBJECTIVE
Determine the cause of cracking observed in returned CP-600 pump housings.

2. EVIDENCE REVIEWED
Returned field parts
Dimensional measurements
Manufacturing lot records
NCR-5521
Material inspection data
Historical thermal-housing lessons learned

3. FINDINGS
Three returned parts contained cracks near a molded transition adjacent to the sealing region.
Two of the three cracked housings had local wall thickness near the lower end of specification.
One returned housing was associated with a production period included in the broader investigation around NCR-5521.
Material identity was confirmed as the released CP-600 material and was not Polymer Grade B.

4. ROOT-CAUSE ASSESSMENT
The investigation concluded that local geometric stress combined with reduced wall-thickness margin and repeated thermal loading contributed to crack initiation. Evidence did not support Supplier-Z or Polymer Grade B as the cause of the CP-600 failures.

5. CORRECTIVE ACTION
Molding-process controls strengthened.
Minimum wall-thickness monitoring increased.
Tooling condition checks added.
Design team initiated geometry robustness review.

6. IMPORTANT HISTORICAL CONNECTION
CP-420 and CP-600 both demonstrated that thermal exposure can amplify weaknesses at locally stressed housing regions, although the materials and direct root causes were different.
