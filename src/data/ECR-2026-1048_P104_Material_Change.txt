PROPOSED SUPPLIER: Supplier-Z

1. CHANGE REQUEST
Replace Polymer Grade A with Polymer Grade B for the P104 pump housing used in the CP-500 electric coolant pump.

2. BUSINESS / ENGINEERING REASON
The proposed material provides improved nominal stiffness and supports a supplier consolidation initiative. Supplier-Z has also proposed improved regional availability and shorter lead time.

3. AFFECTED FUNCTIONS
The housing contains pressurized coolant, supports the motor/pump assembly, maintains sealing interfaces, and must remain dimensionally stable across the specified operating temperature range.

4. INITIAL ENGINEERING CONCERNS
Engineering review identified the following areas requiring assessment:
- long-duration thermal cycling,
- coolant compatibility,
- dimensional stability,
- housing crack resistance,
- supplier material consistency,
- sealing performance after aging.

5. HISTORICAL REVIEW REQUIRED
Before approval, engineering requested a search for previous applications of Polymer Grade B, historical housing cracking, supplier-quality issues involving Supplier-Z, and previous validation results.

6. REQUIRED VALIDATION
Validation shall follow the currently approved coolant-pump validation procedure. The validation team shall prepare DVPR-CP500-P104 and include pressure, vibration, thermal cycling, coolant compatibility, dimensional stability and post-aging leak testing.
