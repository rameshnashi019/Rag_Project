DOCUMENT ID: WR-2025-8821
DATE OPENED: 2025-06-21
PRODUCT: CP-600 Electric Coolant Pump
ISSUE: Coolant Leakage

1. FIELD SUMMARY
A small cluster of customer vehicles reported coolant loss associated with the electric coolant pump.

2. RETURNED PART OBSERVATIONS
Five returned pumps were inspected.
Three showed visible cracking in the polymer housing near the sealing region.
Two showed no external housing crack and were routed for separate seal analysis.

3. VEHICLE USAGE
Reported mileage ranged from approximately 24,000 to 41,000 miles.
Cases included repeated operation in high ambient-temperature regions.

4. INITIAL ASSESSMENT
The observed crack location showed similarity to a high-stress housing region. Engineering requested comparison with manufacturing dimensional records and historical thermal-aging investigations.

5. ACTION
Returned parts sent for detailed failure analysis under FA-2517.
Manufacturing quality records including NCR-5521 requested.
