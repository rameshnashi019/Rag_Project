PRODUCT FAMILY: EV Battery Thermal Management
COMPONENT: P104 Pump Housing

1. PURPOSE
Current approved engineering requirements for P104 coolant-pump housings.

2. MATERIAL
Baseline released material: Polymer Grade A, PA66-GF30.
Alternative materials require engineering change approval and complete material-change validation.

3. KEY REQUIREMENTS
Nominal wall thickness: 3.0 mm
Minimum local wall thickness: 2.85 mm
Operating coolant temperature: -30 C to 105 C
Short-duration peak coolant temperature: 115 C
Maximum normal operating pressure: 3.5 bar
Proof pressure: 7.0 bar
External leakage: None permitted.

4. DURABILITY REQUIREMENTS
No visible cracking, permanent sealing-surface distortion or leakage is permitted after required thermal cycling and vibration exposure.

5. MATERIAL CHANGE REQUIREMENT
A material change requires:
- material property review,
- supplier quality review,
- thermal cycling,
- coolant compatibility testing,
- pressure testing before and after aging,
- dimensional inspection before and after aging.

6. REVISION HISTORY
Rev 6 increased minimum local wall thickness from 2.75 mm to 2.85 mm and clarified post-aging inspection requirements following historical pump-housing investigations.
