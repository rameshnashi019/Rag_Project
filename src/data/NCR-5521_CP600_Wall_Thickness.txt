DATE: 2025-04-09
PRODUCT: CP-600 Electric Coolant Pump
COMPONENT: Pump Housing
SUPPLIER: Supplier-Y
LOT: HY-250401

1. NON-CONFORMANCE
During dimensional audit, local housing wall thickness measured below drawing requirement.

2. REQUIREMENT
Minimum local wall thickness: 2.85 mm.

3. OBSERVED RESULTS
Lowest measured value: 2.71 mm.
11 of 120 sampled housings were below 2.85 mm.

4. CONTAINMENT
Affected lot quarantined.
Additional lots placed on controlled inspection.
100% targeted thickness inspection initiated for suspect inventory.

5. INITIAL CAUSE
Molding-process investigation identified process drift associated with a tooling condition and parameter variation.

6. DISPOSITION
Non-conforming parts rejected. Tooling maintenance and process reset required before normal production release.

7. RELEVANCE
The non-conformance later became part of the CP-600 coolant-leakage investigation because field-return cracks were found in a similar housing region.
