DOCUMENT TYPE: Supplier 8D Corrective Action Report (Synthetic)
DOCUMENT ID: 8D-1208
DATE OPENED: 2022-07-22
DATE CLOSED: 2022-10-14
SUPPLIER: Supplier-Z
MATERIAL: Polymer Grade B (PA66-GF35)
RELATED PRODUCT: CP-420

D1 - TEAM
Supplier quality, materials engineering, manufacturing engineering and Supplier-Z technical representatives.

D2 - PROBLEM
Material characterization following CP-420 thermal-validation failures identified higher-than-expected lot-to-lot variation in moisture conditioning and selected mechanical-property measurements.

D3 - CONTAINMENT
Affected retained lots were placed on hold. Additional certificate review and incoming material verification were introduced for new deliveries.

D4 - ROOT CAUSE
Supplier investigation found that conditioning-control limits were too broad and production records did not consistently distinguish all conditioning histories before shipment.

D5 - PERMANENT CORRECTIVE ACTION
Supplier-Z tightened conditioning limits, added automated recording of conditioning parameters, revised release criteria and introduced additional lot verification.

D6 - VERIFICATION
Three subsequent production lots met the revised supplier control limits. These results verified process consistency but did not independently approve the material for CP-420 or CP-500 housing applications.

D7 - PREVENTION
Supplier control plan and material release checklist updated.

D8 - CLOSURE
Supplier corrective action closed. Any new automotive housing application still requires application-specific engineering validation.
