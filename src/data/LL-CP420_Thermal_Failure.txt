DOCUMENT TYPE: Engineering Lessons Learned (Synthetic)
DOCUMENT ID: LL-CP420-THERMAL
DATE: 2022-11-02
PRODUCT: CP-420 Electric Coolant Pump

1. EVENT
Polymer Grade B was evaluated as an alternative pump-housing material. Initial pressure and vibration testing passed, but cracking occurred during extended thermal cycling.

2. KEY LESSONS
Passing short-duration or individual mechanical tests does not demonstrate long-term thermal durability.
Material changes must be evaluated as a material + geometry + process + operating-environment combination.
Supplier material consistency should be reviewed before release.
Post-aging leak and dimensional testing should follow thermal exposure.
Historical validation failures must be reviewed when the same material is proposed for another pump family.

3. STANDARDIZATION ACTIONS
Extended thermal-cycle review added to material-change checklist.
Supplier quality history added to engineering change review.
Post-aging inspection requirements strengthened.
