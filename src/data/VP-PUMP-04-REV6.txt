1. SCOPE
Current validation procedure for electric coolant-pump assemblies and polymer housing changes.

2. REQUIRED BASE TESTS
Pre-test dimensional inspection
Pressure proof test
Leak test
Vibration durability
Thermal cycling
Coolant compatibility / aging
Post-aging dimensional inspection
Post-aging pressure and leak test
Visual crack inspection

3. MATERIAL-CHANGE REQUIREMENTS
Alternative housing materials require extended thermal-cycle evaluation when historical data indicate thermal-aging sensitivity.

4. SUPPLIER REVIEW
Supplier material-control history and relevant corrective actions shall be reviewed for new material sources.

5. ACCEPTANCE
No visible cracking or external leakage is permitted. Dimensions affecting sealing and structural integrity shall remain within approved requirements.

6. HISTORICAL QUERY NOTE
Rev 6 is the current procedure. Historical projects must be evaluated against the revision effective at the time of their original validation.
