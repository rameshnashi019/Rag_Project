1. SCOPE
Validation requirements for electric coolant pumps and polymer housings.

2. CORE TESTS
Dimensional inspection
Pressure proof testing
Leak testing
Vibration durability
Thermal cycling: standard program up to 300 cycles
Post-test visual inspection

3. MATERIAL CHANGES
Engineering shall review material specifications and repeat affected validation tests.

4. LIMITATION
Rev 4 did not explicitly require extended thermal cycling for every alternative polymer material and did not mandate post-aging dimensional comparison.

5. REVISION STATUS
Superseded by later revisions following validation and field lessons learned.
