PRODUCT FAMILY: EV Battery Thermal Management
COMPONENT: P104 Pump Housing

1. PURPOSE
Defines engineering requirements for the P104 polymer coolant-pump housing.

2. MATERIAL
Approved baseline material: Polymer Grade A, PA66-GF30.

3. KEY REQUIREMENTS
Nominal wall thickness: 3.0 mm
Minimum local wall thickness: 2.75 mm
Operating coolant temperature: -30 C to 105 C
Short-duration peak coolant temperature: 115 C
Maximum normal operating pressure: 3.5 bar
Proof pressure: 7.0 bar
Leakage requirement: No visible external coolant leakage during acceptance testing.

4. DURABILITY
Housing shall maintain structural integrity and sealing performance following pressure, vibration and thermal-cycle validation.

5. REVISION NOTE
Rev 5 was superseded by Rev 6 after engineering review of field and validation experience.
