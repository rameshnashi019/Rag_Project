MATERIAL: Polymer Grade B
GENERIC DESCRIPTION: PA66 with 35% glass-fiber reinforcement
APPROVED SUPPLIER: Supplier-Z

1. INTENDED APPLICATION
Engineering polymer intended for structural under-hood components subject to thermal and mechanical loading.

2. NOMINAL CHARACTERISTICS
Higher nominal stiffness than the current PA66-GF30 baseline.
Suitable for molded structural components when application-specific environmental and durability requirements are validated.

3. PROCESS SENSITIVITY
Material properties can be affected by moisture conditioning, molding parameters and fiber orientation. Supplier processing and conditioning controls are therefore important.

4. APPLICATION LIMITATION
This specification does not by itself approve the material for any specific coolant-pump housing. Product-specific engineering validation is required.

5. QUALITY REQUIREMENTS
Each production lot requires certificate review and compliance with Supplier-Z's revised conditioning-control process introduced after 8D-1208.
