DOCUMENT TYPE: Validation Test Report (Synthetic)
DOCUMENT ID: TR-1845
REVISION: Rev 3
TEST DATE: 2022-06-06 to 2022-06-24
PRODUCT: CP-420 Electric Coolant Pump
COMPONENT: Polymer Pump Housing
MATERIAL: Polymer Grade B (PA66-GF35)
SUPPLIER: Supplier-Z

1. OBJECTIVE
Evaluate Polymer Grade B for use in a coolant-pump housing under pressure, vibration and thermal exposure.

2. TEST SUMMARY
Pressure proof test: PASS
Vibration durability: PASS
Standard thermal cycling, 300 cycles: PASS
Extended thermal cycling, target 500 cycles: FAIL
Post-aging leak test: FAIL on 2 of 8 samples

3. FAILURE OBSERVATION
Fine surface cracking was first observed on one sample after approximately 420 thermal cycles. At 468 cycles, a second sample developed a crack near the housing-to-cover sealing region. Both affected samples showed leakage during post-aging pressure testing.

4. MATERIAL / DIMENSIONAL OBSERVATIONS
No abnormal dimensional condition was recorded before environmental exposure. Microscopic review indicated crack initiation near a locally stressed molded region.

5. CONCLUSION
Polymer Grade B met pressure and vibration requirements but did not demonstrate acceptable margin during extended thermal cycling in the tested CP-420 housing configuration.

6. RECOMMENDATION
Do not release the material for this housing configuration without further material characterization, molding-process review and repeat thermal validation.
